# 💫 About Me:
Good morning, my name is Mahin Pathan. I am an Electronics & Telecommunication Engineer with a B.Tech degree from G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering, Nagpur, with a CGPA of 8.18. I have a strong interest in circuit designing, embedded systems, and core electronics. I have practical experience working with microcontrollers, sensors, analog and digital circuits, and hardware debugging. I have worked on projects such as an Agricultural Drone, Smart Navigation Robot, IoT Home Automation System, and Self-Balancing Robot.<br>I have also gained industrial exposure through internships in transformer manufacturing and organizational technical operations. I am a quick learner with good problem-solving, teamwork, and adaptability skills. I am looking forward to starting my career in a role where I can apply my electronics knowledge and continue developing my technical skills.<br>
